# Privacy Policy for StaySyncGold

**Last Updated:** March 2026

StaySyncGold ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("Customers") and their end-users ("Guests"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our SaaS property management system (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### Personal Data explicitly provided
When you register for our Service, we may ask you to provide:
- Full Name
- Business Email Address and Phone Number
- Billing and Payment Information (processed securely by Stripe)
- Property Details and Configurations

### End-User (Guest) Data
As a Property Management System, you (the Customer) determine what guest data is uploaded to our platform. This typically includes:
- Guest Names, Emails, and Phone Numbers
- Reservation Dates and Payment Statuses
- Identity or Verification documents (if processed through your operations)

We act as a **Data Processor** for your Guest Data. We do not use Guest Data for our own marketing purposes under any circumstances.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected data for various purposes:
- To provide and maintain the Service
- To notify you about changes to our Service
- To provide customer care and support
- To provide AI-driven analytics, forecasting, and reporting natively within the app (powered by Google Gemini)
- To monitor the usage of the Service
- To detect, prevent and address technical and security issues

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers (Sub-Processors)
We may employ third-party companies worldwide to facilitate our Service. The primary sub-processors you authorize us to use include:
- **Supabase / AWS:** For database hosting, authentication, and cloud infrastructure.
- **Stripe:** For financial payment processing.
- **Google (Gemini):** For providing AI insights within the dashboard.
- **Intuit (QuickBooks):** For accounting integrations (if enabled by you).
- **Channex:** For channel manager synchronizations.

These third parties have access to your Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

## 4. Data Security
The security of your data is important to us. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, including Data-at-Rest encryption, Row Level Security (RLS) policies, and TLS for Data-in-Transit. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights
Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California), you may have the following rights:
- The right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you.
- The right of rectification.
- The right to object or restrict processing.
- The right to data portability.

To exercise these rights, please contact us. Note that if you are a Guest requesting data deletion, you must contact the Hotel/Property (our Customer) directly, as they control your data.
