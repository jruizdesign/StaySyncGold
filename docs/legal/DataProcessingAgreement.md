# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Last Updated:** March 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between StaySyncGold ("Processor") and the Customer ("Controller").

## 1. Roles of the Parties
In the context of the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- The **Customer** acts as the Data Controller (deciding why and how Guest Data is processed).
- **StaySyncGold** acts as the Data Processor (processing Guest Data only on behalf of the Controller).

## 2. Scope of Processing
Processor will process Personal Data strictly in accordance with the Controller’s documented instructions, consisting of the operations necessary to provide the StaySyncGold SaaS property management system as outlined in the Terms of Service.

## 3. Confidentiality and Security
### 3.1 Confidentiality
Processor shall ensure that its personnel engaged in the processing of Personal Data are informed of the confidential nature of the Personal Data, have received appropriate training on their responsibilities, and have executed written confidentiality agreements.

### 3.2 Security Measures
Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Personal Data from Security Incidents. These measures include:
- Encryption of Data in Transit (HTTPS/TLS) and Data at Rest.
- Role-based Access Controls (RBAC) and Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure tenant isolation.
- Regular infrastructure security patching and monitoring.

## 4. Sub-Processors
Controller grants a general authorization to Processor to engage Sub-processors. Processor will maintain an up-to-date list of its Sub-processors. Current authorized Sub-processors include:
- Supabase (Hosting / Database / Auth)
- Amazon Web Services (Underlying Cloud Infrastructure)
- Stripe (Payment processing)
- Google / Gemini AI (Cloud APIs for native analytical features)
- Channex (Channel Management endpoints)

If Processor adds or replaces a Sub-processor, Processor will notify the Controller prior to such changes.

## 5. Data Subject Requests
Processor shall, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify Controller if Processor receives a request from a Data Subject to exercise their rights (such as access, rectification, erasure, or portability) under applicable Data Protection Laws. Processor shall not respond to such requests independently but will assist the Controller in fulfilling the request via the Service's built-in functionality.

## 6. Personal Data Breaches
Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach. Processor will take reasonable commercial steps to mitigate the effects and minimize any damage resulting from the Security Incident.

## 7. Return or Deletion of Data
Upon termination or expiration of the Terms of Service, Processor shall (at Controller's election) delete or return to Controller all Personal Data in its possession or control, except as required by law.
