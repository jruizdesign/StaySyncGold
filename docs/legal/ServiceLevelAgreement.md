# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Target Audience:** Enterprise Tier Customers
**Last Updated:** March 2026

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") is a policy governing the use of the StaySyncGold property management service ("Service") and applies exclusively to active accounts operating under Enterprise subscription plans.

## 1. Uptime Commitment
StaySyncGold commits to providing a Monthly Uptime Percentage to the Customer of at least **99.9%** ("Service Commitment"). 

**"Monthly Uptime Percentage"** is calculated by subtracting from 100% the percentage of minutes during the month in which the Service was completely unavailable ("Downtime"). Downtime explicitly excludes:
- Scheduled Maintenance (notified at least 48 hours in advance).
- Outages caused by factors outside of our reasonable control (e.g., natural disasters, acts of war, regional AWS backbone failures).
- Outages resulting from the Customer's equipment, software, or third-party integrations (e.g., QuickBooks or Channex downtime).

## 2. SLA Credits
If the Service fails to meet the Uptime Commitment, the Customer will be eligible to receive a Service Credit as follows:

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit Applied |
| :--- | :--- |
| Less than 99.9% but equal to or greater than 99.0% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Less than 99.0% | 30% of monthly fee |

To receive a Service Credit, the Customer must submit a claim within 30 days of the end of the billing month in which the Downtime occurred. Credits are applied exclusively to future invoices and are not issued as cash refunds.

## 3. Support Response Times
Based on the severity of the issue, we commit to the following initial response times during standard business hours (Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST):

### Severity 1 - Critical Business Impact
*Definition:* The Service is completely inaccessible or a core function (e.g., reservations, payments) is broken for all users, with no workaround.
*Target Response Time:* Within 2 hours.

### Severity 2 - Significant Business Impact
*Definition:* Essential functionality is degraded or broken, but a workaround exists, or the issue affects a subset of users.
*Target Response Time:* Within 4 hours.

### Severity 3 - Minor Business Impact
*Definition:* Non-critical bugs, UI anomalies, or feature inquiries.
*Target Response Time:* Within 24 hours.

## 4. Exclusions
This SLA does not apply to any performance or availability issues:
- Due to factors outside our reasonable control (Force Majeure).
- That result from the use of services, hardware, or software not provided by us, including but not limited to bandwidth issues resulting from your internet provider.
- During Alpha or Beta testing phases of new modules.
